# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this research more accurate and accessible.

## Good contributions
- Correct a claim with a verifiable source or test evidence.
- Add firmware version / BMS part number context to a CAN finding.
- Document unanswered questions or a safe offline validation strategy.
- Improve readability, citations, diagrams, or traceability.
- Report a failed hypothesis or incompatible part with supporting evidence.

Please open an issue before a major restructure.

## Evidence and safety
Label findings **source-reported**, **inferred**, **unverified**, or **project-tested**. Do not represent hypotheses as tested facts. No vehicle conversion has been validated by this project.

Do not submit proprietary firmware dumps, non-redistributable calibration data, private messages without permission, personal data, or instructions to defeat BMS/VCM safety interlocks, contactor permissions, isolation monitoring, or shutdown behavior.

The project is independent and community-led; no contribution implies a safety certification.
